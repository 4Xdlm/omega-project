TO THE DIRECTOR OF THE POLICE FOR JEWISH AFFAIRS SIR, Excuse me if I presume to write to you in person about this, but my husband was taken away at 4 A.M. on 16 July 1942, and as my little girl was crying, they took her at the same time.
