He fetched a slim, gray volume from his library to show me: La Place de l’Étoile by Robert Desnos,5 whose friend he was. Dr. Ferdière had had it published himself, in Rodez, a few months after Desnos’s death in the camp at Terezin in 1945, the year I was born.
