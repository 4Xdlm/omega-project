There were a few clouds. The wind was blowing up above, but down here it was hot. He knocked on the door again with the wooden handle of his whip just to be stubborn, even though he knew that Pedro Paramo wouldn’t open the door until he felt like it. Looking at the lintel of the door, he said to himself, “These black monkeys look nice, whatever it is they are doing here.” Just then the door opened, so he went inside. “Come in, Fulgor.
