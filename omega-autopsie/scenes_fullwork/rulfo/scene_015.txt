We were so alone here, and there was no one else. And the earth has to be populated somehow. Perhaps you might confirm him, when he returns.’ ‘Leave each other. Then he never came back. And that’s why this place is filled with souls, a complete wasteland of people who died without pardon and would never be able to receive it, certainly not from us.
