And I forgot to tell him that someone came here yesterday with the report that he had killed someone. If that goes on…” He sighed and tried to imagine where the cows could have gone now. But he was distracted when he saw that Miguel’s horse was rubbing his nose against the wall. “He didn’t even take the saddle off,” he thought. “He doesn’t care.
