“I a sinner…” his head bent over, as if he could no longer hold it upright. Then he had that dizziness, that sensation of dissolving in thick water, and of lights spinning around; all the daylight was breaking into fragments; and there was a taste of blood on his tongue. Then, “I a sinner…” was repeated again more loudly, and finally he finished: “forever and ever, amen,” “forever and ever, amen,” “forever and ever…” “That’s enough,” he said. “How long has it been since you came to confession?” “ Two days, Father.” There he was again. As if he were surrounded by misfortune. “What are you doing here?” he thought to himself. “You should get up and get some rest.
