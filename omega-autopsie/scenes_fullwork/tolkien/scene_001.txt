His ancestor Bullroarer Took was so huge that he was able to ride a full-sized horse rather than a pony. At the Battle of the Green Fields he once charged full tilt into the ranks of the Goblins from the Misty Mountains and knocked their king Golfimbul’s head right off with his club. (It flew a hundred yards through the air and fell down a rabbit hole, and that is how the game of golf was invented.) This sort of behaviour is considered rather too colourful by usual hobbit standards and it has taken quite some time for the family to recover its reputation. At the age of 50, Bilbo is just heading into a comfortable middle age (for hobbits can live to be 130 years old). He lives alone in a hole in the hillside called Bag End, and is generally well thought of in Hobbiton as a sensible and settled sort of chap. As a youngster he was always running off to search for Elves in the woods and to explore the borders of the Shire.
