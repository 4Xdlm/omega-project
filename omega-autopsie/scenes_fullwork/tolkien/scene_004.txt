Energetic and good-humoured, the pair have the sharpest eyes and the quickest feet in The Company: they are the lookouts, scouts and hunters of the group, always ready to run ahead to spy out the lie of the land or to chase down a rabbit. From an early age they have both been given intensive weapons training, making them swift and able fighters. Kili is tall for a Dwarf, and is both inquisitive and spirited, with a rebellious streak and a twinkle in his eye that can land him in trouble.
