‘The inspector ordered that the bird be searched. One of the agents stalled saying it made him feel sick, and after some fierce pecking another withdrew sucking a bleeding finger’ ITALO CALVINO Born 15 October 1923, Havana, Cuba Died 19 September 1985, Siena, Italy ‘The Queen’s Necklace’ and ‘The Workshop Hen’ were written in Italian and first published in 1993. They were translated into English and published in Numbers in the Dark in 1996.
