But here I am, paying for it anyway. Where is the site? Tell me. Mountains about thirty miles west of here. Gravel road, then a dirt track. I have three men working. Drilling and dynamiting. There were five but two pulled out. Blasting a space of some kind. Out of rock. A room, a cube. I don’t have a name for it. First we cut a passage in. A rough narrow entranceway, cramped, with jutting rock. I make my way through a cold, dank, narrow space. Fifty meters. Natural light only. Bring a flashlight, I guess. I reach the end. What do I find? A chamber, a cubical room. Fashioned out of solid rock. Precise dimensions. A large empty room. Six congruent square surfaces. Painted. Ocher and amber. Old colors. Burnt brick. Lampblack. All six surfaces, every square inch. Painted. Painted. And the paintings. What do I see exactly? You understand this will never happen. It’ll never get that far. I don’t want to describe the paintings anyway. Wouldn’t be able to. I think I know them. Is that right? Yes, I know the paintings. How is that possible? You talked about them. Once or twice only. Middle of the night, we’re in bed, smoking, with the cat on the windowsill, watching. You remember this. Art that’s hidden in a mountain. An incredible, you said, sort of stone enclosure that you would drench with paintings of your dreams. I said this. And the cat. You used to write down your dreams. Your analyst made you do this.
