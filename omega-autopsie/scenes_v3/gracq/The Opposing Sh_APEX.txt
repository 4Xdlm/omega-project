It seemed rather than that it was confined by them that this air had preserved the old walls, like those putrescent swamps which give the pilings driven into them the eternity of stone, so that out of this ancient and immaterial element the gold leaf of the faded ceilings, the heavy leather peeling from the walls, the dense substance of the chiseled tables, the barbarous thrones of raw oak continued to nourish and imperceptibly make lustrous their worn patina, a life which abandoned the comings and goings of the pale shadows still faintly throbbing with the slowed pulse of the winter season: as in certain monuments more deeply perforated than a coral reef and more organically encrusted by the centuries, on which the population instinctively feels that the very survival of certain very old cities
