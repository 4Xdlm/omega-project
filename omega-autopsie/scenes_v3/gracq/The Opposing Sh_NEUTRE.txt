Why did I ever come back? I hate these bare walls—never anything to look at but the waves and the fogbanks ...” Her voice was in my ear. “It’s as if we were in a plundered port, and all the tide gates were broken. We seem to be cut loose in these huge rooms. As if the ship were slipping anchor ...” “But you’re the one who wants to keep all these doors open, Vanessa. It always seems to me as if we were sleeping in the street.” “Poor Aldo!” She caressed my hair distractedly. “...
