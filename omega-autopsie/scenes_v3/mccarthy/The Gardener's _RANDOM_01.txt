She takes hold of the bedlinen on the far side of the bed and pulls it back. He half raises up in the bed to stop her. R OBERT No. She jerks away the covers. He struggles. Her hat is crushed. His legs lie revealed. The injured one is loosely swathed in bandage through which discolored stains have seeped and his whole lower leg and foot are perfectly black. She turns her head aside at the odor. He falls back sobbing. She recovers him and puts her hand to his face. M RS GREGG I know it’s very hard. I know. I’ll stay with you. The kitchen.
