Rueldo looked to me for support, but I was having none of it; Arlysa was captain. Besides, I was afraid of that thing out there. Angrily, Rueldo said to both of us, "Don't you realize how important a find this could be?" Arlysa raised her eyebrows fiercely. "How important?" Rueldo shifted his gaze toward the viewport. His pupils dilated visibly, his nostrils flared, his breath seemed to slow. What was happening to him, what was that thing doing? "Rueldo, talk to us!" I shouted. He ignored me. "If you don't start explaining, I'm going to set a course back to Cervantes right now," Arlysa said quietly, her voice almost metallic in its flatness. For a moment he seemed to ignore her as well. Then, without turning, he said, "The quasars, you know—they've never been fully explained." That was an answer? I shook my head in confusion. "What are you talking about?" I said. "Quasars are exploding galaxies. They're billions of light-years away—and what we observe are events from an early stage of the universe. Every school child knows that.
