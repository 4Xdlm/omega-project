THE cavalry’s tanks and units of the mounted dragoons were maneuvering along the road. These were small units, for there was not enough room to spread out between the Meuse and the frontier, and the armored formations —if the rumors were to be believed—were being kept quite far behind the lines, in the Champagne camps; but the Meuse cavalry, in any case, was to operate in the Ardennes, strung out along these threadlike forest roads which the service paths connected so fragmentarily: apparently the essence of these exercises that sometimes awakened Les Falizes with the sudden roar of motors was to set the comb’s teeth along the same line.
