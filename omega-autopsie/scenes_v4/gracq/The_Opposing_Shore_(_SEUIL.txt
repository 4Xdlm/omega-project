Belsenza grew perfectly still, and his eyebrows drew together, as if at a difficult question. “My dear Observer, it was you who started it ... I like things to be black and white myself. But as soon as I begin a report, the pen falls from my fingers. Once you try to take matters in hand, they immediately assume a new form. As if they were afraid of being caught, of being checked ... as if people were afraid they couldn’t get away. Or that the rumors might stop ...” Belsenza made a face, irritated and mocking. “It all comes down to ... if you want to bring it down ... to very little, to nothing at all. To less than nothing.
