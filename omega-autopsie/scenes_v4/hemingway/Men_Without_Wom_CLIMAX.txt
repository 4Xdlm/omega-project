He set down his suitcase and knocked on the door. There was no answer. Manuel, standing in the hallway, felt there was some one in the room. He felt it through the door. “Retana,” he said, listening. There was no answer. He’s there, all right, Manuel thought. “Retana,” he said and banged the door. “Who’s there?” said some one in the office. “Me, Manolo,” Manuel said. “What do you want?” asked the voice. “I want to work,” Manuel said. Something in the door clicked several times and it swung open. Manuel went in, carrying his suitcase. A little man sat behind a desk at the far side of the room. Over his head was a bull’s head, stuffed by a Madrid taxi-dermist; on the walls were framed photographs and bull-fight posters.
