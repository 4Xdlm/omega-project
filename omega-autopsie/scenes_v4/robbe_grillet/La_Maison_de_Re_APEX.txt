It is just at this moment that the British police have burst into the large salon of the Blue Villa, but this episode has already been described in detail: the short harsh whistle blast which abruptly stops the orchestra and the murmur of conversation, the iron heels of the two soldiers in shorts and short-sleeved shirts that ring on the marble floor in the sudden calm, the dancers who have frozen in the middle of a step, the man standing with one hand extended in front of him toward his partner still half-turned away, or the two partners facing each other but looking in different directions, one to the right and the other to the left, as if their attention had been attracted at the same moment by diametrically opposed events, other couples, on the contrary, keeping their eyes mutually fixed on their feet, or their bodies united in a motionless embrace, and then the careful search of all the guests,
