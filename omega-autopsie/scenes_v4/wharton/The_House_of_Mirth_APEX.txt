I have paid my card debts, of course, but there is hardly anything left for my other expenses, and if I go on with my present life I shal be in horrible difficulties. I have a tiny income of my own, but I'm afraid it's badly invested, for it seems to bring in less every year, and I am so ignorant of money matters that I don't know if my aunt's agent, who looks after it, is a good adviser." She paused a moment, and added in a lighter tone: "I didn't mean to bore you with al this, but I want your help in making Judy understand that I can't, at present, go on living as one must live among you al . I am going away tomorrow to join my aunt at Richfield, and I shal stay there for the rest of the autumn, and dismiss my maid and learn how to mend my own clothes." At this picture of loveliness in distress, the pathos of which was heightened by the light touch with which it was drawn, a murmur of indignant sympathy broke from Trenor.
