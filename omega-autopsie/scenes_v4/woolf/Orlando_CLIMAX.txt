‘Here! Shel, here!’ she cried, baring her breast to the moon (which now showed bright) so that her pearls glowed like the eggs of some vast moon-spider. The aeroplane rushed out of the clouds and stood over her head. It hovered above her. Her pearls burnt like a phosphorescent flare in the darkness. And as Shelmerdine, now grown a fine sea-captain, hale, fresh- coloured, and alert, leapt to the ground, there sprang up over his head a single wild bird. ‘It is the goose!’ Orlando cried. ‘The wild goose …’ And the twelfth stroke of midnight sounded; the twelfth stroke of midnight, Thursday, the eleventh of October, Nineteen hundred and Twenty Eight.
