The key turns. The door opens. The smell hits her first.

Mother's smell. Still there. Still alive in the dead air.

Nothing has moved. Nothing. The cup on the table. The newspaper folded at the crossword. Half finished. Always half finished.

She enters. She closes the door behind her. The sound echoes. Empty. Full. Both.

The apartment breathes without breathing. Waits without waiting.

Her feet know the floor. The creaking board by the window. The loose tile near the kitchen. Mother complained about that tile. Always complained. Never fixed it.

Now no one will fix it.

She moves toward the bedroom. Stops. Moves again. The bed is made. Mother made it that morning. The last morning. Made it and died.

The drawers wait.

She opens the first one. Handkerchiefs. White. Pressed. Folded in squares. Perfect squares. Mother's hands folded them. Those hands. Gone now.

Second drawer. Letters. Bundles of letters tied with ribbon. Blue ribbon. Faded blue. She knows these letters. Knows them without reading. Father's letters. From the war. From before. Before everything changed.

Her fingers touch the ribbon. Mother's fingers tied this ribbon. Untied it. Read the letters. Tied it again. How many times. How many nights.

She doesn't untie it.

Third drawer. Photos. Black and white. Yellow at the edges. Herself as a child. Smiling. Gap-toothed. Mother behind her. Young mother. Beautiful mother. Before the lines. Before the silence.

There. That photo. Christmas morning. The doll with yellow hair. She remembers the doll. Remembers the morning. Remembers mother's hands smoothing her dress. Gentle hands. They could be gentle.

The notebook is underneath. Brown leather. Worn smooth. She opens it. Mother's handwriting. Careful. Precise. Lists. Always lists. Groceries. Appointments. Birthdays.

Her own birthday. Circled in red. Every year circled in red.

The last entry. Tuesday. "Call Marie." That's her. Marie. She didn't call. Was busy. Always busy. Too busy.

Mother waited for the call.

The tears come. She hadn't planned them. Hadn't wanted them. But they come anyway. Fall on the notebook. On mother's careful writing. Blur the words.

She sits on the bed. Mother's bed. The springs creak. Mother's weight made them creak. Now her weight. Different weight. Same sound.

The apartment holds her. Holds her grief. Holds mother's absence. Holds everything that was. Everything that won't be.

She closes the notebook. Places it back. Closes the drawer. The photos. The letters. Everything back where it was.

Where mother left it.

She stands. Walks to the door. Turns back. The apartment watches her. Waits for her return. Knows she will return. Tomorrow. Next week. Until she can't anymore.

Until she becomes the one who is waited for.

The door closes. The key turns. The silence remains.

Mother's silence. Forever now.
